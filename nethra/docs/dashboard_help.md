# Nethra Dashboard Documentation

## 1. Executive Summary

Nethra is a high-precision **Mathematical Intelligence Engine** and Strategic Dashboard designed to identify voter volatility and the "moveable middle" at the booth and constituency level. Rather than relying on traditional, delayed polling, Nethra operates as a real-time, geo-fenced sentiment engine.

### Key Features
*   **Hyper-Local Sentiment Mining:** Continuously ingests civic grievances, news events, and localized issues across social media and public platforms.
*   **AI Spam Filtering & Validation:** Employs advanced Frontier AI models to automatically filter out bot farms, repetitive hashtags, and promotional spam, ensuring only authentic civic signals are processed.
*   **Geospatial Tracking:** Visualizes 234 Assembly Constituencies, 39 Parliamentary Constituencies, and 200 GCC Wards on interactive 3D maps, highlighting areas with fluctuating voter sentiment.
*   **Target Generator:** Automatically drafts high-converting, region-specific campaign material (WhatsApp forwards, Twitter posts, Instagram captions) based on verified local issues.

### How to Use for Election Campaigns
1. **Identify Flashpoints:** Use the interactive maps to spot constituencies or wards glowing red/orange—these indicate high issue salience and voter volatility.
2. **Understand the "Why":** Select a specific unit to read the verified, authentic civic issues (e.g., delayed bus stands, infrastructure failures) driving the sentiment.
3. **Deploy AI Campaigns:** Navigate to the "Target Generator" to review localized messaging generated by the AI, and deploy it to Meta or Google Ad networks to capitalize on the identified local grievances.

## 2. Technical Summary

### Score Calculations
*   **Authenticity Score (0.0 to 1.0):** Calculated by a Large Language Model (LLM) analyzing the text of scraped content. High scores (0.7+) represent genuine, localized human grievances or credible news. Low scores (<0.3) represent generic political spam, SEO articles, or promotional content.
*   **Geographical Relevance Score (0.0 to 1.0):** An LLM-derived score evaluating how tightly the issue is bound to the target constituency. If an article mentions a statewide issue without local impact, it scores low. If it names a specific street, bus stand, or local factory in the constituency, it scores 1.0.
*   **Favorability & Saliency (MRP Projections):** Baseline favorability is calculated using Multilevel Regression and Poststratification (MRP). Real-time event saliency modifies these baseline priors, effectively "swinging" the predicted percentages dynamically.

### Data Sources
*   **Web Data Mining:** Open-source scraping of Google News RSS and old.reddit.com (bypassing strict modern API paywalls while maintaining high data quality).
*   **Census Data:** Incorporates demographic structures (Age, Gender, Occupation, Social Group) from the 2011 Census of India.
*   **Electoral Rolls:** Utilizes public, anonymized counts from Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) databases to construct accurate poststratification demographic frames without PII.

### Mathematical Model
Nethra utilizes **Multilevel Regression and Poststratification (MRP)**. 
1. **Regression:** A Bayesian hierarchical model estimates opinion (favorability) for specific demographic profiles, pooling data across similar regions to stabilize estimates for sparse data cells.
2. **Priors:** Behavioral psychology traits (like loss aversion to local infrastructure failures) are encoded as mathematical multipliers ($\gamma$) in the model.
3. **Poststratification:** The modeled opinions are projected onto the actual demographic counts of a specific booth or constituency to yield a highly accurate, weighted final projection.

### System Architecture
1. **Data Ingestion:** Parallel crawlers (`mine_verified_sources.py`) extract raw text and URLs from news aggregators and social channels for a target list of 400+ constituencies.
2. **AI Authentication & Geoclassification:** The raw feed is routed through the Gemini 1.5 Flash API. A zero-shot prompt forces the LLM to output a strict JSON schema containing the `authenticity_score`, `geo_relevance_score`, and categorized `issue`.
3. **Data Storage:** High-scoring items are committed to a local SQLite Database (`nethra_campaign.db`), ensuring fast read operations for the dashboard.
4. **AI Content Generation:** For highly verified issues, a secondary LLM call generates platform-specific ad copy (WhatsApp, X, Meta) tailored directly to the localized grievance.

### Technologies Used
*   **Frontend UI:** Streamlit, Plotly (for charts), PyDeck (for 3D maps).
*   **Backend & Data Engine:** Python (Pandas, SQLite3, Concurrent Futures for multithreading).
*   **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API (gemini-1.5-flash).
*   **Data Scraping:** `requests`, `BeautifulSoup4`, `feedparser`.
