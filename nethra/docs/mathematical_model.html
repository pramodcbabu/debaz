Nethra: The Mathematical Foundation

1. The Booth Volatility Index (BVI)

The BVI is a composite score (0 to 1.0) calculated for every polling booth. A high BVI indicates a "volatile" booth where a significant number of voters are undecided or "swingable".

\[ BVI = \alpha \cdot M_{hist} + \beta \cdot S_{vol} + \gamma \cdot I_{unrest} \]
  • \(M_{hist}\) (Historical Margin): Normalized historical victory margin. Lower margins contribute to higher BVI.
  • \(S_{vol}\) (Sentiment Volatility): Calculated from AI survey data. Represents the variance in sentiment over a 30-day window.
  • \(I_{unrest}\) (Issue Unrest): The density of specific local grievances (extracted via NLP) compared to regional averages.

2. The "Dirty Data" Anomaly Detection

To combat fabricated cadre reports, Nethra uses a Cross-Validation Model. If a local cadre report claims 90% support in a booth, but the AI Survey Engine and historical trends show 40% support, the cadre data is flagged as "anomalous" and its weight is penalized.

3. Causal Inference & ROI Verification

To prove ROI to political clients, Nethra employs a Control vs. Treatment methodology at the booth level.

graph LR subgraph Experiment Design A[Identify 100 Volatile Booths] A --> B[Random Assignment] B --> C[Treatment Group: 50 Booths] B --> D[Control Group: 50 Booths] end subgraph Intervention C --> E[Nethra Active: Targeted Ads + AI Interventions] D --> F[Status Quo: Traditional Campaigning Only] end subgraph Measurement E --> G[Vote Day Results] F --> G G --> H[Calculate Average Treatment Effect - ATE] end
\[ ATE = E[Y_1 - Y_0] \]

Where \(Y_1\) is the vote share in Treatment booths and \(Y_0\) is the vote share in Control booths.

4. Swing Voter Clustering

Using K-Means clustering on multidimensional sentiment vectors (Age, Issue-Priority, Support-Intensity), we identify the "Psychological Profile" of the swing voter. This allows for the generation of content that resonates specifically with their value systems.
