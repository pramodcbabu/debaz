📖 TVK System Architecture & Math Whitepaper
Comprehensive technical documentation, KNN geospatial interpolation proofs, and mathematical models

1. System Architecture Overview

Nethra AI operates on a multi-tiered data engine capturing real-time social sentiment, RTI filings, legislative assembly debates, and ground-level voter issue surveys across Tamil Nadu.

2. KNN Geospatial Baseline Interpolation

For parliamentary constituencies and GCC wards where historical TVK baseline figures are algorithmically estimated, Nethra uses K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) geospatial mapping based on the closest 2026 Assembly Constituencies.

3. Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Trajectory Model

Historical trends use a smoothed EMA trajectory with smoothing factor λ = 0.35:

S_t = (λ × Target) + ((1 - λ) × S_(t-1))

4. Spam & Bot Farm Filter Engine

Incoming social media data is passed through an automated NLP filter measuring sentiment score, duplicate text frequency, and posting velocity to block coordinated bot farm attacks.
